Digital Footprint
Risk Analyzer

A project designed to help people understand the risks their online habits and personal information can create. It brings four models together into one overall digital-risk assessment.

01

Digital habits
Looks at online behavior and habits.

02

Password strength
Analyzes a password's strength.

03

Digital footprint
Explores personal information present online.

04

Password prediction simulation
Demonstrates why personal information should not be used in passwords.

The four models contribute to an overall risk percentage, turning separate observations into one understandable result.
